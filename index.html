Why Agentic AI > Simple Dashboard
🔄 Autonomous Decision-Making
Agents take actions automatically based on thresholds — no human needed to click every button. A simple dashboard would just show data.
🤝 Agent Collaboration
Risk Agent feeds Drainage Agent, which feeds Civic Response Agent. Multi-agent coordination produces compound intelligence impossible with isolated modules.
💡 Explainable Reasoning
Each agent explains its reasoning — contributing factors, confidence scores, action rationales. Not a black box — officers understand WHY the AI recommends what it does.
